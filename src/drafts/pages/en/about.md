## Jean-Philippe Bourgeon 

> Papa Bear · Public-sector IT Technical Director &amp; Deputy Director · Systems Enthusiast

My work spans IT systems and organizations: understanding how they work, designing them, and making decisions about how they evolve. I write here in a personal capacity, not on behalf of my employer.

I like finding out how systems work and imagining how they could work differently. That curiosity stays with me whether I’m writing code or working with a team. I want to be able to explain a choice and check that it meets the need. Keeping things simple helps us stay in control when we have to maintain or change what we’ve built.

## Why this blog

What changes for a team when a task it used to do is automated? Here I use my projects and experiments to explore how technical choices shape the way people work.

I explain what shaped those decisions and where they ran up against limits in practice. That gives you a way to judge what holds up in your own setting, what you’d adapt, and where you’d make a different choice.

I use AI agents to explore ideas, draft, translate, and review the text. I decide what to say and approve each publication.

## Projects and contributions

I also like testing ideas in code. Design, usage, and maintenance constraints force me to make precise what looked straightforward on paper.

I publish open-source work, notably through the GitHub organization [at-rama](https://github.com/at-rama).
