# editorial-verify

Examine the candidate article independently of its authoring pass. Verification is evidence for an authorial decision, not that decision.

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `standards/editorial.md`, `standards/voice.md`, the exact FR/EN candidate files, and every applicable `rationale` link in their frontmatter. Resolve `.42p/rationale-units/<unit-slug>/capture.md` against the actual repository, including any additional explicitly identified source units and author decisions needed for the declared review. A Capture is non-authoritative and must not corroborate itself.
2. Resolve initial publication versus revision and the exact FR/EN candidate paths, canonical target paths, effective URLs and (for a revision) unchanged canonical baselines. With the **one tested shared** normalization/projection implementation from `AGENTS.md`, record `SHA256(N(candidate))` and `SHA256(N(P(candidate, baseline)))` for each language. `N` excludes only top-level `draft`/`unlisted`; temporary and canonical `slug` values remain significant in their respective identities. `P` may remove visibility, restore the prior canonical slug and target path, but must make no other editorial repairs. Use Git blob IDs for applicable standards/Captures and (for revisions) canonical baselines; do not invent additional source-version hashes. Confirm effective routes, relative assets/links and that no unapproved metadata is altered. Require both locales and reproducible target bytes for a publication-ready `PASS`; otherwise mark the review `INCONCLUSIVE`.
3. Examine the three alignments independently:
   - **Rationale fidelity:** preserve adopted orientation, rationale, alternatives, conditions, uncertainty, scope and authorial authority; detect invented commitments, omissions, strengthening, weakening and drift. Inspect available original sources when necessary to substantiate a material claim; distinguish source limitations from an established mismatch. Where no Capture is applicable, mark this axis not applicable, not failed or proven.
   - **Editorial standard:** accuracy and attribution, separation of fact and inference, meaningful structure, intelligibility, historical context, terminology, functioning links and code examples to the extent verifiable. Do not judge whether the article is worth publishing, sufficiently original, impactful or useful: these are the author's editorial choices.
   - **Voice standard:** personal and measured expression, natural connected prose, proportionate abstraction, and no invented experience, conviction, or promotional posture.
4. Check substantive FR/EN parity, including claim strength, exceptions, technical examples, outbound references, effective canonical URLs and visibility metadata. If a requested preliminary review examines only one locale, state the limited scope; it cannot certify bilingual readiness.
5. For each applicable Capture, make a concise, auditable **material coverage mapping** between relevant stable handles/source passages and article passages: `COVERED` (faithfully represented), `EXCLUDED` (intentionally out of this article's scope, with reason), `MISSING` (materially necessary but absent or distorted), or `INCONCLUSIVE` (insufficient evidence). Include inverse checks for important article claims unsupported by Captures, author decisions or independent evidence. Count states arithmetically, optionally showing `COVERED / (COVERED + MISSING + INCONCLUSIVE)`; explicitly exclude justified `EXCLUDED` items from this denominator. Selection and classification are **semantic judgments**, not deterministic measurements or a complete inventory of every Capture line; never claim an objective quality score or require 100% coverage. For an article with no relevant Capture, mark the coverage axis `N/A`.
6. Record for each applicable axis `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `INCONCLUSIVE`. A material contradiction, including a materially necessary `MISSING` coverage element, is `FAIL`; insufficient evidence for a material claim is `INCONCLUSIVE`. A justified `EXCLUDED` element is not automatically a defect. Overall `PASS` requires every applicable axis and bilingual parity to pass. Never infer approval from a passing review. Cite file passages and evidence for material findings; avoid fabricated numerical quality grades.
7. Write a compact report at `.42p/editorial-reviews/<canonical-article-slug>.md`. Its machine-readable YAML frontmatter records `article`, `mode` (`new`/`revision`), `candidates.fr/en` (`path`, `content_sha256`), `targets.fr/en` (`path`, `content_sha256`), `references[]` (`path`, `git_blob_sha`) and overall `result` (`PASS`/`FAIL`/`INCONCLUSIVE`). For revisions include `baseline.fr/en` (`path`, `git_blob_sha`). In the body record the per-axis verdicts, concise coverage mapping with justifications/counts, significant defects/unknowns, and `Human editorial approval: not granted by this report`. A substantive candidate change, a changed canonical projection, reference revision or baseline revision makes the report stale; a visibility-only change does not. Keep only the useful current report: Git records history. Commit a publication-ready `PASS` report **before** the publication commit; the independent deployment gate checks its prior existence and integrity, without treating it as authorization.
8. Present the report and material findings to the author for an explicit decision concerning the exact FR/EN candidate and projected target versions. The report is evidence, not a request that an agent approve its own output; `PASS` does not institute publication authority.
9. Do not change the article, sources, standards, canonical baseline or visibility while verifying. Report blockers and the exact scope of conclusions. Do not ask the author to perform routine checks.

The report establishes only the correspondence and quality supported by the examined evidence. It does not institute a decision, prove unexamined source truth, or authorize deployment.
