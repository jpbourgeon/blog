# editorial-explore

Read `AGENTS.md`, the editorial standard and the material relevant to the
question. Start from the author's request and established decisions. Work in
dialogue and follow the parts of the problem that merit investigation.

Clarify the thesis, intention and reader. Test premises and arguments against
objections, implications and trade-offs. Compare angles and possible
progressions. Investigate sources and missing evidence. Consider concrete
examples, code, analogies or figures where they improve understanding. Make
trade-offs between depth, clarity and scope explicit when they affect a choice.

Link factual claims and examples to verifiable sources. Distinguish established
facts, hypotheses and author decisions. Identify missing evidence and the
questions that would change the result.

Offer options and critiques for the author's judgment. Leave unadopted
proposals open. If the author wants to retain material decisions, questions or
evidence for resumption, use `42p-capture` to reconcile the relevant deltas.

Keep the exchange as light as the inquiry allows. Exploration requires no
formal deliverable, OpenSpec CLI, prescribed plan or FR/EN candidate.
